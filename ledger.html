HOW TO READ THIS

A section is a block on a plate: a passage, a table, a record or a note. Figures and quotations are not counted as claims. The level is the one the plate itself gives the section.

A section with no source is not necessarily wrong, but it is where a reader should ask what it rests on. The ledger is generated at every build, so it cannot drift from the plates.
