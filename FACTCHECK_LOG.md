# FACT-CHECK LOG — RUSSIAN CHTHONIC ATLAS

Started 2026-11-15, as part of the “Illustration expansion and historical fact-checking” pass. This log records what was actually checked, what was changed and what was not done. Status words follow the brief: VERIFIED, PARTIALLY VERIFIED, DISPUTED, UNVERIFIED, INCORRECT, INTERPRETATION.

## 1. Phrase scan (done)

Scanned for the brief’s generalising formulas (traditionally, throughout Russia, ancient Slavs, in Russian folklore, originated in pagan times, pre-Christian, pagan, always meant, universal, people believed, all the dead, never, always, everywhere, every, survival) in the English text of all 24 plates (prose, tables, captions) and in concepts, lexicon, method, objects, timeline, places, routes and prologue data.

- Plates: 16 hits (pagan 5, universal 3, always 2, every 2, everywhere 2, pre-Christian 1, never 1). Each read in context.
- Other data files: 7 hits (concepts 1, lexicon 1, method 3, objects 2, one of them printed twice by the scan).
- Result: no unsupported generalisation of the kind the brief lists was found by this scan. In every plate hit the formula is either reported as an author’s argument and attributed (the pagan-survival reading on plates C, T, U, V), used inside a table as a limit (“not universal”, “cannot show … a name used everywhere”), used to mean Church-wide (Meat Fare and Trinity Saturdays, from Gumerov) or describes a source’s own method (Ryko, the Middle Volga highway survey).
- This scan finds wording, not errors of fact. A passing scan does not mean the facts were verified.

## 3. Not done

- No source was re-read in full for page-level checking. Where a source was read in this pass it was read through an open-access PDF summary or a preface extract, and the limitation is in its bibliography entry.
- The Russian text was not scanned with the same phrase list.
- Chronology dates, the lexicon, the object catalogue, FIELD ARCHIVE and READING ROUTES were scanned for phrases only, not checked entry by entry against their sources.
- Image captions were not checked image by image for object, place, date, author and licence beyond what the Commons record states. The provisional role field in the asset register is not a fact-check.
- Image evidence status is missing for 13 images placed through plate layouts (see node build/check_credits.mjs).
- No second independent source was searched for the contested topics the brief names (pledged dead, pre-Christian origin, Radonitsa and Semik, flooded cemeteries). Plates E, T and U present the disagreements they have found; they do not claim the disagreements are exhausted.
