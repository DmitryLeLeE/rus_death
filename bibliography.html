    https://elib.rgo.ru/handle/123456789/216718 — accessed 2026-11-15.

    USED FOR Zelenin’s compilation of reports that people who died an ‘unnatural’ death were buried away from the churchyard, including the two local cases used on plate N: a forest ravine near Orkino (Saratov uezd) and a ‘deaf forest ravine’ in Silinskaya volost (Simbirsk guberniya); his term ‘zalozhnye’ for the ‘unclean’ dead and his reading of them as rusalki, as the later studies read for this atlas report them.     LIMITATION Only part of the book was read: chapter 4, ‘Commemoration of the zalozhnye at Semik’ (sections 34–36), through a summary of an online copy of the 1995 reprint, not line by line. Page numbers for the authorities he cites (Dobrovolsky 1908, Georgievsky 1894, Veshchomov, Glushkov, Zelenin 1904) are as that reading gave them and are second-hand: the originals were not seen. Elsewhere the term, the category and the rusalka reading are taken from later authors who cite him, and the two local cases on plate N were read as excerpts. The Orkino report concerns half-Russified Mordvins, not Russians. The compilation is over a century old; the category is Zelenin’s construction, and later scholarship disputes its coherence and the rusalka derivation.
